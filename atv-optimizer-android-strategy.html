ATV Optimizer — Android app feasibility & go-to-market

A phone/tablet app that debloats an Android TV over Wi-Fi ADB · freemium with a one-time Pro unlock · synthesized from 24 sources, 23 verified claims
Build it. It's technically feasible today, demand is real, and your architecture is unusually well-positioned for the port.

The whole thing hinges on one crux decision — how you implement the Android-11+ ADB pairing transport — because the obvious off-the-shelf library (libadb-android) is GPLv3, which fights a closed-source paid app. Resolve that and the rest is a known quantity. Two landmines to route around: Google Play removal risk and the "Android" naming rule.

1Can it even work?High confidence

Yes. A non-rooted Android phone can act as an ADB client to another device over Wi-Fi, including the modern Android-11+ wireless-debugging pairing flow. The hard part is already solved in shipping code.

  • The pairing flow is solved. libadb-android (powers the well-known App Manager) exposes the whole Android-9+ TLS pairing as a single call — AdbConnectionManager.pair(host, port, pairingCode) — plus mDNS auto-discovery of the target (_adb-tls-connect._tcp). It connects to a remote adbd and runs arbitrary shell: commands. That's exactly your pm disable-user / settings put / cmd package surface.
  • Pure-Rust ADB exists but is missing the one piece you need. adb_client (MIT, mature) talks to devices directly over USB/TCP with no adb-server — but it does not implement the Android-11+ TLS pairing-code flow. dadb (JVM) is the same story (pairing is an open request). So pairing is the part you build or borrow.
  • Android-TV wrinkle. The pairing-code UI needs Android 13+ on the TV (phones are 11+). Your Shield-on-Android-11 fleet uses the older path — so the app needs both: pairing-code for TV-13+, and legacy adb connect ip:5555 / tcpip for older TVs that expose it.

2Why your codebase is the right starting pointHigh confidence

This is a port, not a rewrite — because you already split pure logic from I/O.

Reuse as-is

  • The pure Rust engine (safety classification, debloat plans, do-not-disable gate) ships on Android via Tauri 2's mobile plugin path + JNI. No logic rewrite.
  • The Svelte 5 UI is already responsive SPA — Tauri 2 runs it on Android.
  • Your AdbDriver trait is the seam: the phone just gets a new driver backed by the wireless transport instead of SubprocessAdb.

Build new

  • The transport: there's no adb binary on a phone — the app is the client. New pairing + connection layer (the crux below).
  • Pairing UX for non-technical users (enable wireless debugging on the TV, enter code).
  • Touch-first nav + the on-TV enablement walkthrough.
The payoff of the pure-engine discipline: the audited safety layer and the curated app lists move over untouched. You're swapping the bottom 20% (transport), keeping the valuable 80% (what's safe to disable, plans, snapshots).

3The crux: the pairing transport & a license trapDecide this first

The fastest library is libadb-android — but it's GPLv3 (copyleft). Link it into a paid, closed-source app and you're obligated to release the whole app's source, which guts a paid model (anyone can rebuild and redistribute free). This is the single most important call.

OptionEffortLicense fit for paid appVerdict
A. Kotlin + libadb-androidLowest — it's done✗ GPLv3 forces source releaseGreat for a free/OSS build; bad for a closed Pro tier
B. Clean-room ADB-TLS pairing (Rust, contributed to adb_client, or own Kotlin)Highest — implement SPAKE2 + TLS pairing✓ Permissive / yoursRecommended — keeps it all-Rust, permissive, and reusable on desktop too
C. Legacy-only (adb connect 5555, no pairing)Low✓ adb_client is MITShips fast but only works on TVs exposing port 5555 — weaker UX, narrower device support

My lean: ship a v1 on Option C (legacy connect via the MIT Rust crate — covers Shield-on-11 and any TV with network debugging, fully permissive) to validate the market, while building Option B (pairing-code support, ideally upstreamed to adb_client) for the broad TV-13+ audience. Avoid A for anything you want to charge for.

4Distribution: sideload-first, Play cautiouslyMedium confidence

Google Play is a real removal risk for this category — don't bet the product on it.

  • Play's Device & Network Abuse policy broadly bans apps that "interfere with, disrupt… or access in an unauthorized manner… other devices… networks or services." An app that disables/uninstalls apps on another device over ADB sits squarely in the blast radius. Comparable tools (App Manager, ADB AppControl) live off Play (F-Droid, own site, XDA).
  • Note "We get consent, so we're fine" is not a safe harbor — that specific compliance angle was refuted in verification. Don't rely on it.
Recommendation: sideload-first — own-site APK download, exactly like your desktop app already distributes unsigned. Add Amazon Appstore / F-Droid (if you OSS a free build). Treat Play as an optional, conservatively-worded secondary listing you can afford to lose — never the only channel and never the payment chokepoint.

5Naming: you're right, with a caveatHigh confidence

Google's brand guidelines: "Android" cannot appear in an app name (only "for Android"), and "Android TV" is prior-approval-only. Their own example bans "Android TV Streaming Stick."

  • ❌ "Android TV Optimizer" — not allowed.
  • ✅ "ATV Optimizer" — your instinct is the safe one. A coined brand (e.g. a unique name) is even safer for trademark + ASO, with "for Android TV" only in the description/subtitle, not the title.
  • Bonus: dropping "Shield" also frees you from Nvidia's mark and matches a tool that already spans Google TV + generic Android TV.

6Monetization: one-time Pro unlockMedium confidence

  • On Play: a one-time "managed product" via Play Billing. Fee is 15% on the first $1M/yr — but only if you enroll (default is 30% from dollar one), and the US/EEA/UK fee structure is changing 30 Jun 2026, so re-check before relying on numbers.
  • Off Play (where you'll mostly live): sell a license key via Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad / Polar / Stripe (they act as merchant of record, handle VAT). Validate the key in-app (online check + offline grace). No Play cut, no Play removal risk. Trade-off: a sideloaded paid APK will be cracked by some — accept it; price for the honest majority.
  • Price band: the research did not pin hard benchmarks (flagged as an open question). Category norm for a one-time utility Pro unlock is roughly $5–10; I'd start ~$6–8 and test. Validate this before launch.

8Competitor: atvtoolsUnverified — re-check

This is the weakest part of the research and worth a focused follow-up before you size the gap.

  • Verification refuted (0–3) the claim that atvtools is the phone-drives-TV-over-network model, with one source saying it's iOS. But there's also a Play listing dev.vodik7.atvtools — which suggests an Android app does exist. Net: its exact platform, pricing, distribution, and reviews are unconfirmed.
  • What is clear: the feature space is demand-validated. atvTools' described features (app disable/hide, cache clear, shell, sideload, file push, button remap, remote) heavily overlap yours; ADB AppControl ships a dedicated "ADB TV" variant with a paid debloat-recommendations tier. People pay for this.
Your differentiation regardless of where atvtools lands: the safety story (audited do-not-disable gate, snapshots/undo, curated per-device lists) + a genuinely no-PC, clean pairing UX. "Debloat your TV from your phone without bricking it, and undo anything" is a sharper promise than the grab-bag tools.

9Roadmap & rough effort

1
Spike the transport (1–2 wks). Prove a phone can adb connect a Shield over the network via the MIT Rust adb_client and run pm disable-user. De-risks everything.
2
Engine + UI on Tauri-mobile (2–4 wks). Wire the pure Rust engine via JNI, get the Svelte UI touch-usable, implement a new AdbDriver over the spike transport.
3
Pairing-code support (the long pole, 3–6 wks). Option B — implement Android-13+ TLS pairing (SPAKE2 handshake), ideally upstreamed to adb_client so desktop benefits too. Or defer to v1.1 and ship legacy-only first.
4
Monetization + distribution (1–2 wks). Free/Pro gate, license-key check (Lemon Squeezy), own-site APK, conservative store listings.

Solo, part-time: a credible legacy-connect v1 in ~6–8 weeks; full pairing-code + polish a month or two beyond. The pairing transport is the only genuinely uncertain estimate.
