## Defaults and recovery limits

Accepted entries start Keep and are excluded from automatic safe selection. Missing packages are skipped; disabled packages are not disabled again; Restore can deliberately enable an installed disabled app without default selection. [PackageManager user-disabled state](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/content/pm/PackageManager#COMPONENT_ENABLED_STATE_DISABLED_USER) supports conditional re-enable, not restoration of sign-in, authorization, paid access, settings or deleted data. Exact current Play listings set play_store=true, retaining existing separately confirmed Uninstall eligibility. These are Disable suggestions, not disable-only controls. [Google TV app installation guidance](https://support.google.com/androidtv/answer/6121463?hl=en) does not guarantee recovery of deleted preloaded APKs: reinstall depends on compatible device/store/region/account and app availability. [Google subscription guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) distinguishes uninstalling from cancellation. MGM+ billing is not cancelled by disabling/uninstalling.

Pure engine, system/protected/caution, last-HOME, setup/network, user-0, selection and confirmation gates remain authoritative. Known-name recognition is never action authority. No transport, platform safeguards or legacy verdict changes.
