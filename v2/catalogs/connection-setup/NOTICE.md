# Connection setup: source attribution

`connection-setup.json` adapts the menu paths and connection notes that owners
wrote into Gathin23/cleanyourtv, copyright (c) 2026 Gathin, at commit
`7b7666ad05a3a62ba25768c51fbf06e9c4e67da5`, under the MIT License. The full
upstream notice is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), copied verbatim. Retain this
attribution and that notice when redistributing the file or a substantial part
of it.

Each variant's `sources` lists the upstream files (per-model `debloat.txt` and
`README.md` notes under `examples/`, plus `docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md`) its steps
come from; the generic steps come from the upstream `README.md` and
`PROMPT.md`. Local modifications: the notes are grouped by brand and model,
reworded into short steps, and translated from "Settings > A > B" into a list
of menu names. Nothing was added that a source doesn't state; where a source
gives no step, the app shows the generic steps instead.

man15h/tellyscope-devices (MIT) was checked at commit
`59406e5feb035f80a336a86799527ed2f4d02e15` and holds no menu paths, so nothing
here comes from it. No Tellyscope app code, text or assets are used.
