# Known quirks on this firmware:
# - The saved Wi-Fi network belonged to setupwraith (`dumpsys wifi | grep -o 'cname=[^ ]*'`). Forget the
#   network and join it again in Settings first; afterwards it belongs to android.uid.system and
#   setupwraith can be disabled without the TV dropping off Wi-Fi.
# - The Vietnamese tab names on the stock Google TV home ("Màn hình chính", "Ứng dụng") and its TCL tab come
#   from com.tcl.partnercustomizer, not from the system locale (en-US here): with it disabled the stock home
#   shows "Home" and "Apps".
# - Signing in to a Google account right after the list made Play Store install VTV Go, HBO Max and iQIYI
#   (installer com.android.vending). They are ordinary apps: uninstall them, and turn off auto-update in
#   Play Store settings.
# - TclAppBoot leaves the launcher's key services unbound until `appops set com.gathin.tvhome
#   APP_AUTO_START allow` followed by `appops write-settings` (docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
# - Disabling dreamx: set screensaver_components to com.gathin.tvhome/.ClockDream first.
# - Remote buttons with launcherx disabled, checked on the real remote: same codes as the C69B in
#   tcl-g10-google-tv/. With INPUT_KEY_SERVICE=1 and HOME_KEY_SERVICE=1, settings_key_keycodes 83 and
#   app_keys 4065:com.google.android.youtube.tv, 4062:com.netflix.ninja, Home, Input, Settings, YouTube,
#   Netflix and the mic (Assistant) all work.
