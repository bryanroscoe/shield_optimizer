# Panasonic 4KTV-JUP (Jupiter_PA, MediaTek), Android TV 11 (API 30), firmware 4.501 / RTM2.210929.137
# armeabi-v7a, 1920x1080, ~1.7 GB RAM (about 50 MB free at idle before debloating).
# This is a Panasonic/MediaTek Google TV build: there is no PatchWall or other OEM content stack.
#
# Stock launcher:  com.google.android.tvlauncher
# Live TV app:     com.mediatek.wwtv.tvcenter  (Panasonic's TV UI; keep it, the Inputs menu opens it)
# Never disable any com.mediatek.* package: they are the tuner, input and HDMI stack.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/panasonic-4ktv-jup/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/panasonic-4ktv-jup/debloat.txt
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Status: disabled together on one TV on 2026-10-18 and checked with remote-key injection and screenshots
# (launcher, HDMI/AV switching, Live TV). Nothing broke in that testing, but this list had under a day of
# real use when submitted: treat it as a starting point, and re-enable anything you miss.
#
# Three apps claim HOME here (stock launcher, a third-party launcher, FallbackHome). After
# installing launcher/, `cmd package set-home-activity` is needed before the stock one is disabled.
