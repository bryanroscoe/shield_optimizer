# Notes from this firmware:
# - No packages were disabled before this list. Default IME is already Gboard and every package
#   below exists on this build.
# - The older Android TV home (com.google.android.leanbacklauncher) is still installed next to
#   tvlauncher, like on MSSP3 PI.4663. Both disabled here and Home kept resolving to the new launcher.
# - Screenshots of Live TV and HDMI come back empty (0 bytes); check the screen with
#     dumpsys activity activities | grep mResumedActivity
#   and the playing input with dumpsys tv_input (inputId under sessionStateMap).
# - Live TV closes itself after about 25 s on an HDMI port with nothing connected and drops back to Home.
# - ADB over the 5 GHz Wi-Fi stalls for 20-120 s now and then, mostly while the screensaver runs.
#   Wake the TV (KEYCODE_WAKEUP) and retry.
# - Private DNS dns.adguard-dns.com works for ad and tracker domains; it does not block YouTube ads.
# - Left alone on purpose: com.mediatek.wwtv.tvcenter / com.mediatek.tvinput (HDMI + tuner), mitv.service,
#   com.mediatek.hotkey.dispatcher and com.xiaomi.android.tvsetup.partnercustomizer (remote hotkeys,
#   Netflix button), com.google.android.tv.remote.service (phone remote), com.xiaomi.mitv.mediaexplorer
#   (USB Media Player), com.xiaomi.mitv.updateservice (firmware updates), Play Store, Play Services,
#   Google Assistant, Chromecast and the keyboard.
